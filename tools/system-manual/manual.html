1

The system at a glance

One server in the office, opened in a browser on any PC or phone.

Sheehan QMS gives every customer a numbered ticket, shows the line on the TV in the waiting area, and follows the customer from the sales counter, through each department that loads their order, to the security check at the gate. Everyone sees the same live picture, refreshed every few seconds.

Words used in the system

Word What it means
Ticket / queue number One customer's visit. Its number is the first letter of each type of order, then a count that starts again at 01 every day: RS 01 is Roofings and Steel, TB 04 Tire and Battery, ST 08 Stainless. A * in front marks a priority order, ** a TBA priority one.
Type of order Roofings, Roofings Accessories, Steel, Stainless, Chassis, Tire, Battery, Hardwares. A ticket can have several.
Hand over Sales finish with the customer and send them on to the departments. Sales never call customers to the display; the departments do.
Area and department Where an order is loaded. The loading crew works four areas (Roofings, Steel, Logistics, Chassis); Service and Hardware are departments with one area each. See the table in section 2.
Call, Start, Done The three presses a department makes for each area: call the customer to it, start once the truck is there, done once it's loaded.
Security check The last stage: a guard checks what the customer leaves with, and presses Verified or reports a problem.
Payment status How the order is paid: Vale, Paid, Terms, COD or Partial. Set by sales or the cashier, at any time.
Role The kind of account a staff member has. It decides which pages they see and what they can press (section 3).

Side roads

When Who What to do
The customer needs nothing loaded Sales Done on My counter. The order goes straight to the security check.
The salesperson who made the ticket is away Another salesperson Assist on My counter takes the customer over; they can then hand them over (and get the point).
A served customer comes back Sales Returning on My counter. Where they go next depends on whether they were handed over: see Returning customers, the next pages.
Called, but the truck isn't there yet Department Call again repeats the call; Back to waiting returns them to the line.
Sent away with the wrong receipt or item Department Recall calls them straight back to an area already done, any time today (even after the gate), with a reason. Only that area is loaded again, and the order is checked at the gate again.
The guard finds a problem Security Report a problem: Missing, Over the quantity or Stolen, with a note.
A ticket was made by mistake Sales, admin Delete on Orders & history. It waits under Deleted, where it can be restored.
Picking the items up on a later day Sales Set the pick-up day on the ticket. On that day it comes back into the line as RS 01 · PICK-UP · Nov 11, keeping its first number. See Returning customers.

Returning customers

A customer comes back for their order. Which way they go depends on two questions.

"Returning" means one of two things in the app: a customer who comes back the same day after their order was done (the Returning button on My counter), or one who comes back on a later day for the items (a pick-up, set when the order is taken). Follow the chart from the top.

Good to know Returning works on today's tickets only. A customer who was done at the counter gives back that salesperson's point until they're done again; a handed-over customer's point stays with the salesperson. The Order status board shows a returning customer as "Returning customer, in line".

3

The roles, one by one

What each kind of account is for.

Admin home: Dashboard

  • Everything: every page and every step.
  • The day's orders by status and the sales graph on the Dashboard.
  • Accounts, the display's video and announcer, the receipt printer.
  • Purges deleted tickets for good.

Sales home: My counter

  • Customer order: takes the order and prints the ticket; the Order sheet; Orders & history.
  • My counter: hands the customer over (or Done, or Assist).
  • Cashier page: sets the payment status.

Doesn't call customers to the display; can delete a ticket but not purge it.

Loading home: Loading

  • One shared line for the whole crew, for Roofings, Steel, Logistics and Chassis.
  • Call, Start loading, Done; Back to waiting; Recall.
  • History tab, and a Crew tab with who works which area and who's online.

Sees a customer only once sales has handed them over.

Service · Hardware home: their page

  • The same as Loading, for their own area: Service (Tire, Battery) or Hardware (Hardwares).
  • Hardware's words are "Start preparing" and "Prepared by", since items go over the counter.

Only their own department's customers and history.

Security home: Security

  • Every order today, each step, and what the customer bought: invoices, items, who loaded it.
  • Verified, or Report a problem with a note.

No payment, salesman or other sales details; nothing else to change.

Cashier home: Cashier

  • Every day's orders and how each is paid; one tap to change it.
  • By product tab: each type of order on its own.

No items, runner, checkers or other sales details; nothing to call.

Inventory home: Dashboard

  • The live queue and the Order status board: every order today and where its customer is.
  • SheeChat and My account.

No work page of its own yet; nothing in the queue to press.

Developer home: Dashboard

  • The team that builds the app. SheeChat's Staff concerns tab: answers what staff write from Message developer, and their reports.
  • Order status, Deleted tickets (Purge), Accounts (not admin accounts).

Nothing in the queue they can call, change or save.

Points Staff earn one point per customer served: the salesperson when they hand the customer over, and whoever is serving the customer when the order is marked done. Reopening the ticket in the same visit takes the point back.
4

Signing in and the Dashboard

The start of every shift.

  1. Open the Sheehan QMS app, or the office address followed by /staff.
  2. Type your username and password, then Sign in.
  3. You land on your home page (section 3). The menu on the left has only your pages.

Each browser tab has its own sign-in, so two people can share a PC in two tabs. You stay signed in until you press Sign out (bottom left), for up to 30 days, or a week without using the app.

Forgot your password? Ask an admin to set a new one on Accounts. After many wrong tries the PC has to wait a few minutes.

Dashboard every role

Your own customers are tinted and marked "(you)". Further down, admins get the sales graph, developers the open Staff concerns, and everyone else a link to each of their pages.

5

Sales: Customer order and My counter

Step 1 and step 2 of the ticket's path.

Take the order Customer order · Queue ticket

  1. Customer name and Type of order (tick one or more; the ticket's letters come from these).
  2. Invoices: each invoice number with the type of order it's for. + Add invoice for more.
  3. Sales details: salesman (you, already filled in), sales team, payment status, time in and out, and a pick-up day if they'll collect later. All optional but the salesman.
  4. The Checker tab has the 1st and 2nd checker for Roofing, Construction and Hardware/TBA/Chassis; blank ones print as a line to write on.
  5. Press Serve. The ticket gets its number and prints. Test print prints the form's ticket without taking the order.

Check the ticket before it prints Ticket preview

A half-filled form isn't lost Going to another page and back keeps what you typed, until you press Start over. To print a ticket again later, pick it in View queue ticket at the top right.

Print the ticket half long bond · 6.5 × 8.5 in

Tickets print on half a sheet of long bond paper: long bond (8.5 × 13 in) cut across its middle makes two sheets of 6.5 × 8.5 in. Insert a sheet vertically into the printer, the short 6.5 in side first. The paper size is set once on the PC with the printer; after that every ticket prints right.

Step 1 · Open the print window

Step 2 · Set the paper to 6.5 × 8.5 in, once

  1. In the print window, open More settings.
  2. Click Printer properties. The printer's own window opens.
  3. At Document Size (or Paper size), pick User-Defined.
  4. Choose inches, then type 6.5 for the width and 8.5 for the height. Name it Ticket 6.5x8.5, press Save, then OK, and OK again. Keep Portrait.
    Width 6.5, never 8.58.5 wide is the sheet lying across, and the ticket comes out cut off.
  5. Back in the print window, Paper size now shows the 6.5 × 8.5 size.
  6. Margins: None. Scale: Default, never Fit to page. Then Print.
Only onceThe browser remembers the size for this printer. From then on just press Serve, check the preview says 1 sheet of paper, and Print.

What comes out

If you see Do this
Only part of the ticket is on the paper The paper size is still Letter or long, or the sheet went in lying across. Insert it vertically and set User-Defined 6.5 × 8.5 (Step 2).
The ticket is small, or in a corner Margins None and Scale Default in the print window.
The printer's window has no User-Defined Settings → Ticket paper → Open the setup manual (PDF), part 4: it adds a Ticket 6.5x8.5 paper size in Windows instead.

Hand the customer over My counter

  • Hand over to loading: after the talk with the customer. They join the line of every department their order needs.
  • Done: for a customer with nothing to load. They go straight to the security check.
  • Assist: on a coworker's ticket while they're away; you can then hand the customer over.
  • Hand over is greyed out on someone else's ticket until you press Assist.

The Order sheet Customer order · Order sheet

  • Click any cell but the date, number and status to change it; it saves as soon as you leave the cell, and the others see it within a few seconds.
  • Invoice numbers sit under their department: Roofing, Construction & hardware, TBA, Chassis, Others. Shift+Enter puts several in one cell.
  • Add row makes a new ticket once it has a name, a type of order and an invoice number. Add column adds a column of your own.
  • Click one of today's queue numbers to open its ticket.

Orders & history Customer order · Orders & history

  • Status: set Waiting, Returning customer or Done. Only admins can set Now serving, which calls the customer.
  • View ticket to print it again, Edit to change it. Every edit that changes something is kept, as "Edited by Maria Santos · GIGA · 12:54 PM" with what changed.
  • Delete moves a ticket made by mistake to Deleted, where it can be restored. On All tickets, tick several to delete them at once.
  • Export to Excel saves the orders between two days.

The queue display

What the customers see on the TV in the waiting area.

  • It needs no sign-in: open the office address on the TV's PC, with nothing after it, and make it full screen.
  • It refreshes every few seconds by itself. Each new call is announced aloud, at the volume set on Display & printer.
  • A customer leaves Now serving once their area is done, and the display once their loading is finished.
7

The queue display on a busy day

When many customers are waiting and several counters call at once, the numbers stack.

On the screen What it means
The big number (RA 15) The newest call, with the name and where to go, gate included. It's also read out loud. When the next counter calls someone, that number takes its place.
The stack on the right (H 14, T 13, S 11) Everyone else still at a counter, newest first, each with their area and gate. One number per counter, so several are served at the same time.
+3 more The stack shows 3. More customers called than that are still being served; they're counted here instead of shown. As those in the stack finish, the hidden ones move up into it.
Next 8 of 17 Up next has room for 8 rows. The line here is 17 long, so only the first 8 show; the rest move up as those ahead are called. The red number (S 02) is the very next customer.
Numbers out of order Up next runs in ticket order, but skips anyone already called: T 13, H 14 and RA 15 are at counters, so after RS 12 comes C 16. A pick-up comes after that day's walk-ins, and Returning next to a name means they came back today.
Tell customers: if your number isn't on the screen yet, you're still in line; keep your paper and watch the big number. You'll also hear your number called.
8

Security and Cashier

Step 4 of the ticket's path, and the payment status along the way.

The gate check Security

  1. Find the customer: search by name, ticket number or invoice number, or read the ticket they hand you.
  2. Check the truck against the invoices and quantities shown.
  3. All as it should be: ✓ Verified. The order is done.
  4. Something wrong: Report a problem, pick Missing, Over the quantity or Stolen, and write what. The report stays on the order in red, on Orders & history too.

The filters along the top (In progress, In line, At sales, Loading, Security check, Done, All) show the whole day. On a phone each order is a card.

How each order is paid Cashier

  • Tap Vale Paid Terms COD Partial to set it. It saves at once and shows on the ticket as "Changed by Cashier → Rosa Bayad".
  • It can be changed again, but not cleared.
  • Filter by status (or Not set), or search by name, ticket number, invoice or date.
  • The By product tab shows each type of order on its own, with only its invoices.
  • Sales and admins have this page too; the cashier sets it when the salesperson isn't around.

Everyone's tools

Order status, SheeChat, My account, and the admin's pages.

Order status board

Use it to answer "where is my customer?" without calling anyone. Sales and admins have it beside the order form; the departments and inventory on their Dashboard; everyone else behind the Order status button at the top right.

SheeChat and Message developer

  • Paste or attach a picture with your message. "Seen" shows once they've opened it.
  • Click someone's photo or name to see their profile; Send a message on a profile opens your chat with them.
  • Message developer (the second tab) goes to the whole developer team: say what went wrong, or use the guided buttons to report a bug, a system concern or a feature idea. Their answers come back there.

Accounts admin · developer

  • Make an account: username, name, password (8 or more characters) and role; a sales team for sales, an area (Roofings, Steel, Logistics, Chassis) for the loading crew.
  • Change someone's role or password: they're signed out everywhere and sign in again.
  • Someone leaves: switch the account off. The customers they served stay on record. Once off, Drop account removes it for good; their name stays only on the tickets and points they earned.
  • Developers can do all this for every account but admin ones. Nobody can drop their own account or the built-in admin.

My account every role

Your photo and cover photo, name, employee ID and department, as coworkers see them on your profile. The pencil on your Dashboard profile opens it too.

Display & printer admin

Setting up a PC or phone itself (the certificate, installing the app, the ticket paper) is in Settings, for every role; the separate Device setup manual walks through it.

10

Good to know

The rules the system keeps, and the usual questions.

Question Answer
Why did today's numbers start at 01 again? Queue numbers restart every day, by the server's clock. Yesterday's tickets are all still on Orders & history (All tickets) and the Order sheet.
Why is Call greyed out? The customer is in one place at a time. Someone else has them now; the button says who. Wait until they finish.
Why can't the loading crew see my customer? They only see customers sales has handed over. Press Hand over to loading on My counter.
I pressed Returning but the customer isn't in my Waiting list. They were handed over before, so they went straight back to the department's line, tagged Returning. There's nothing for sales to do; the department calls them.
A ticket was deleted by mistake. It's on Orders & history → Deleted. Press Restore: it's back in today's line as waiting.
Can a deleted ticket be erased for good? Only by an admin or developer, with Purge. A copy of the database is saved first, and the points earned on it stay.
Who changed this order? Every change is kept with the name, team and time: "Edited by …" on the ticket, "Changed by Cashier → …" for the payment.
The payment status was set wrong. Tap the right one on the Cashier page. It can be changed any number of times, but not left blank once set.
A red "Connection lost" bar is showing. The PC can't reach the server. Check the network; the bar goes away by itself when it's back, and nothing typed is lost.
Something isn't working. Write on SheeChat → Message developer, with a screenshot if you can. The developer team sees it and answers there.
